# Flipped (2010)

## About the Movie
*Flipped* is my favorite movie because it tells a heartwarming story about friendship, family, and growing up. The movie follows **Juli Baker** and **Bryce Loski** as they learn important lessons about life and relationships.

## Main Characters
- Juli Baker
- Bryce Loski
- Richard Baker
- Patsy Loski

## Why I Like It
1. Inspiring story
2. Relatable characters
3. Meaningful lessons
4. Unique point of view

### Favorite Lesson
> Never judge a person based on first impressions.

The movie taught me that:
- True beauty comes from a person's character.
- Honesty is important in every relationship.
- Respecting others helps build trust.
- It is important to see the whole picture before making judgments.

## Favorite Scene
Juli's love for the sycamore tree showed how important it is to appreciate the things that make us happy.
